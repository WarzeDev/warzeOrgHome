My new Tool: BruteCraft

BruteCraft is a helpful tool for WynnCraft that helps with a pretty complicated system in the game: Crafting.

In WynnCraft, you can combine hundreds of different items to create gear and consumables with purposes ranging from combat, movement, survivability and more.

BruteCraft helps you figure out the best combination of items to yield the best result possible using a evolutionary algorithm.
